ABOUT

Our project aims to introduce students to the historic space devices and spacecraft launched by NASA. These devices played important roles in space exploration and achieved great things that became part of space history. They also helped build the foundation for many of the technologies and space missions we use today, even though their missions have ended and they are no longer in use. We present this idea in a fun and interactive way through a Space Exploration Museum. It takes students on a exciting journey to discover these historic devices, learn their stories and achivements, and understand how they helped shape space engineering and made today's space missions possible. The experience is not just about reading information. Each devices has its own collection of content, including shor, specially created videos that tell its story, explain its missions, and highlight its most important achivements in a simple and interesting way. The museum also provides detailed information abotut each device, along with a complete library of useful files, documents, and references for the different needs. Whether students simply want to learn about a device, explore its engineering and technical details, or use it for scientific research, they can find helpful resiurces in one place. In addition, the museum brings together NASA's official videos related to each device and its mission, making it a complete starting point for learning, exploration, and research into the history of space exploration and engineering.
